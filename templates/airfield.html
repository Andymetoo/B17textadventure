A NOTE FROM OPERATIONS

A few minutes. A lasting decision.

Our objective is 60 campaign points in 12 operations. Balance valuable targets against keeping the detachment ready.

We receive 4 supplies and 1 spare part before each new operation. Larger formations deliver more, but cost more and expose more crews.

You don't need to stay on duty. Missions and assigned ground work finish while you're away. Nothing else launches without you.

Read the commander's briefing
  • One formation can be away at a time. Send as many available aircraft as you can afford.
  • Crews make every airborne decision. There are no response timers or midair orders.
  • Repairs restore condition to 100% in 45 minutes. One maintenance bay.
  • Crew recovery reduces fatigue by 55 in 45 minutes. One recovery assignment at a time.
  • Conditions and briefing remain unchanged while you are away, apart from work you authorized.
  • The opening sortie takes two minutes. Later flights take two or three hours.
  • The first tour is a playable prototype. Fast-forward below lets you test the loop now.
